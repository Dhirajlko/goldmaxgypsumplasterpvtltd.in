ENGINEERING ADVANTAGES

The Superior Science of Goldmax Gypsum

How switching from traditional river sand plastering to Goldmax Gypsum transforms construction economics, speed, and finishing quality.

100% Water Conservation

Traditional cement plaster requires 14 to 21 continuous days of water curing. Goldmax Gypsum cures through natural chemical crystallization with zero water curing, conserving millions of liters of scarce city water.

Mirror-Smooth Paint Ready

Dries to an immaculate, non-powdery, bright white surface. You can apply primer and paint directly, completely bypassing two expensive coats of wall putty and labor sanding.

Zero Shrinkage Cracking

Gypsum does not experience volumetric shrinkage upon hydration. Unlike cement-sand which frequently develops unsightly map cracks and hairline fissures, Goldmax walls remain perfectly intact.

Natural Fire Barrier

Gypsum contains approximately 21% chemically bonded crystal water ($CaSO_4 \cdot 2H_2O$). In a fire, it acts as a built-in sprinkler system, delaying heat transfer for up to 2 hours.

Low Thermal Conductivity

Features thermal conductivity ($\lambda$) of just 0.25 W/mK compared to 0.72 W/mK for cement plaster. Keeps indoor rooms noticeably cooler in Indian summers, cutting AC energy bills.

50% Dead Load Reduction

With an applied dry density of 700 kg/m³ versus 1600+ kg/m³ for sand cement, it significantly decreases building dead load, lowering structural steel and foundation requirements in multi-storey towers.

STEP-BY-STEP METHODOLOGY

The Goldmax 5-Step Application Process

Engineered for swift, error-free plastering with standardized site protocols.

01

Surface Preparation

Clean the masonry substrate of loose mortar, dust, grease, and efflorescence. Pre-wet high-absorption AAC blocks lightly with clean water.

02

Bonding Keying (RCC)

On smooth RCC ceilings and columns, apply a single uniform coat of Goldmax Bond-It. Allow 24 hours to cure without hacking.

03

Scientific Mixing

Pour potable water into a clean tub, then add Goldmax Plaster in 1 : 1.25 ratio. Allow to slake for 2 minutes and mix with a mechanical paddle.

04

Single Coat Application

Apply firmly in a continuous upward pass with a hawk and steel trowel. Rule off with an aluminum straight edge to the desired 10-15mm thickness.

05

Trowel Polishing

As the mix stiffens (approx. 20-30 min), finish with a clean damp sponge and trowel to achieve an unblemished, glossy paint-ready surface.
