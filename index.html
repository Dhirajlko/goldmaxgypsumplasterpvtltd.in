APPLICATION MANUAL

THE 5-STEP SITE EXECUTION PROTOCOL

Engineered for swift, mistake-proof plastering across residential, commercial, and industrial structures.

01

Substrate Prep

Clean dust, loose mortar, grease, and efflorescence. Pre-wet high-absorption AAC blocks lightly to regulate suction.

02

Bond-It Keying

On smooth RCC ceilings and shear walls, roll a single coat of Goldmax Bond-It. Cures in 24 hours without hacking.

03

Mechanical Mixing

Add Goldmax powder into clean water (1 : 1.25 ratio). Allow to slake for 2 minutes and mix with a paddle stirrer to a lump-free paste.

04

One-Coat Trowel

Apply firmly in an upward pass with a hawk and steel trowel. Rule off with an aluminum straight edge to the desired 10-15mm thickness.

05

Mirror Polish

As the mix stiffens (approx. 20-30 min), finish with a clean damp sponge and trowel to achieve an immaculate, paint-ready surface.
